# Product boundary — what CARACTER is building, and what it is not

Status: internal decision record. Adopted 2026-10-27.

## The boundary

The public site is a **Phase 0 marketing site**: static, five languages, zero
dependencies. It describes a workflow. It does not transact.

Everything that would need a licence, a payment, an order or a signature lives
behind a separate application that does not exist yet. Nothing on the public
site may imply otherwise.

## What is explicitly out of scope until there is a paying customer

- Multi-tenant dashboard, SSO, any authenticated area
- Automated royalty calculation or payout
- Platform payment collection or merchant-of-record behaviour
- TikTok / Meta data pulls via API
- Open third-party IP submission pipeline (no legal capacity to review it)
- Any AI agent that approves a licence or places an order

## What the first paid transaction must prove

One licensed outcome, end to end, with documents:

1. A rights record that is specific enough to refuse a request.
2. A written licence or service agreement with a signature and a payment.
3. Either 3–10 approved short videos, or one sample from a verified factory.
4. A settlement statement produced by hand, with the sales basis shown.

If the first transaction cannot produce those four artefacts, the platform
argument is not yet supported.

## The single test that settles the central claim

Let a territory expire on one SKU. Attempt both a factory order and a creator
campaign that depend on it. If either succeeds, the rights seam does not exist
and must not be claimed on the site.
